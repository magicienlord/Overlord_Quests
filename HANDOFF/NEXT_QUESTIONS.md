# Immediate V5 Continuation Questions

These are the unresolved civilization decisions from the last pre-handoff design message supplied by the Overlord after the rollback.

Do not author the affected V5 civilization sections until the Overlord answers them.

## 1. Should NEUTRAL be a genuine terminal civilization resolution?

Current proposal awaiting decision:

After a civilization's main local problem/authority arc is resolved, the final authored resolution may explicitly record one of the following anchor-local outcomes where appropriate:

- leave the anchor independent → `NEUTRAL`;
- force submission → `SUBJUGATED`;
- deliberately destroy it → `DESTROYED` / `HOSTILE` as appropriate to that civilization.

Rationale already presented:

- “choosing not to conquer them” becomes an actual remembered resolution rather than leaving the questline hanging;
- state changes remain deliberate authored player actions rather than passive disposition drift;
- terminal resolutions have narrative meaning.

Additional proposal awaiting decision:

- once a final resolution is chosen for that canonical anchor, it remains permanent rather than freely switching later.

Need explicit Overlord answer: **approve, modify, or reject**.

## 2. Gnumu ancestry: reveal or withhold the truth?

Established facts:

- Gnumus descend from Gluttony-altered Halflings of the Mellow Hills/Halfling lands;
- Gnarl/the Overlord can know this;
- local Gnumu knowledge may remain limited/absent.

Proposal awaiting decision:

The Gnumu arc eventually uncovers concrete Mellow Hills/Gluttony evidence and then gives the Overlord a remembered historical choice:

- reveal the truth to the Elder Shaman, causing the canonical settlement to learn its Halfling ancestry; or
- withhold it, leaving the Gnumus themselves ignorant.

This choice would be:

- independent of civilization disposition;
- not automatically Good/Evil;
- remembered for later dialogue/world-state purposes.

Need explicit Overlord answer: **approve, modify, or reject**.

## 3. Dwarf–Kobold rivalry: optional shared cross-civilization chain?

Established facts:

- both Dwarves and Kobolds remember the rivalry;
- the rivalry is still active/current;
- individual civilization dispositions remain separate;
- the Overlord may exploit, broker, redirect or profit from inter-civilization conflict through authored quests when appropriate.

Proposal awaiting decision:

Create one **optional shared cross-civilization chain** that unlocks after discovering both the Golden Hills/Golden Halls successor hold and the designated Kobold Den.

Possible authored directions presented in principle:

- favor one side;
- force an arrangement;
- extort both;
- worsen the dispute for profit/advantage.

The chain would **not** be required to finish either civilization's own main arc and would not merge their terminal disposition states.

Need explicit Overlord answer: **approve, modify, or reject**.
