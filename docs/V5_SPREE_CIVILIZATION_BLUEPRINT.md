# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Spree Civilization Blueprint

Status: WORKING V5 CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY

Date: 2026-09-16

Authority relationship: this document is a focused civilization blueprint under `docs/V5_CAMPAIGN_SYSTEM_AUTHORITY.md` and `docs/V5_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM_AUTHORITY.md`. It records explicit V5 decisions approved by the Overlord and franchise-backed Spree civic themes.

## 1. Canonical anchor

The Villager civilization anchor is present-day Spree.

Spree is not a fixed pre-authored coordinate. One qualifying generated Villager settlement becomes canonical present-day Spree when the player deliberately starts the civilization arc there.

A suitable local Villager is bound to the authored civic role:

```text
Mayor of Spree
```

The Mayor title continues source-faithful Spree civic vocabulary. It does not claim that one unchanged municipal institution survived intact across the intervening centuries.

The exact Villager eligibility filters and deliberate anchor-start signal remain technical/objective details for the next source pass. Mere entry into a village or incidental Villager interaction is not sufficient.

## 2. Political spine

The approved Spree political problem is civic viability and dependency.

The central question is:

```text
Can present-day Spree secure its own food, economic life and protection,
or do those civic functions become materially dependent on the Overlord?
```

This model is grounded in franchise Spree, where civic leadership, refugees, farms, food supply and settlement survival were explicit political concerns.

V5 translates those themes into present-day Villager systems rather than recreating the original Overlord I quest sequence literally.

## 3. Required civic pillars

Spree uses three approved provider/function categories:

```text
food and agriculture
commerce and specialist work
Guard Villager security
```

These are political pillars, not fixed profession names yet.

The detailed source pass must select concrete Villager professions, Guard Villager roles and trackable actions only where they materially express one of these functions.

V5 must not create filler providers merely to represent every Villager profession.

## 4. Mayor

The Mayor is:

- the civilization starter or final anchor authority once the deliberate start flow has bound Spree;
- the civic representative who receives evidence about Spree's viability or dependency;
- the final authority for `NEUTRAL` and `SUBJUGATED`;
- one of the indispensable civic targets in the approved `DESTROYED` route.

The Mayor remains a Villager-based authored role. V5 does not require a new custom species or profession merely to express the title.

## 5. NEUTRAL route

The independent route proves that modern Spree can remain viable without becoming an Overlord dependency.

Approved structure:

```text
anchor locked as present-day Spree
-> complete meaningful food/agriculture content that strengthens local self-sufficiency
-> complete meaningful commerce/specialist content that demonstrates a functioning civic economy
-> complete meaningful Guard Villager security content that establishes local defensive continuity
-> demonstrate that those functions can continue without Overlord ownership
-> final civic settlement with Mayor
-> explicitly recognize Spree as independent
-> NEUTRAL
```

The route is affirmative independence, not merely positive Villager relations.

Ordinary trading, Hero of the Village, raid outcomes or any other native state do not automatically select `NEUTRAL` unless the final authored route explicitly uses them.

## 6. SUBJUGATED route

The submission route turns the same civic pillars into dependencies on the Overlord.

Approved structure:

```text
anchor locked as present-day Spree
-> identify the food/agriculture function and make a selected critical part materially dependent on Overlord resources, access or protection
-> identify the commerce/specialist function and establish a selected critical dependency or obligation
-> identify the Guard Villager security function and establish that local safety materially depends on Overlord intervention, resources or authority
-> record only the specific provider obligations actually required by the route
-> present the resulting dependency structure to Mayor
-> Mayor accepts Overlord supremacy while remaining civic administrator of Spree
-> SUBJUGATED
```

This is not a hidden sidequest score. The route must name and prove the exact dependencies that create the political result.

Subjugation preserves Spree as a functioning settlement under Overlord supremacy.

## 7. DESTROYED route

The destructive route deliberately breaks Spree's civic and defensive continuity.

Approved structure:

```text
choose destructive route
-> turn knowledge of Spree's civic functions against the settlement
-> remove the Mayor
-> destroy or irreversibly break the exact indispensable food/economic/defensive functions selected by the source-backed objective pass
-> present-day Spree ceases to function as the canonical polity
-> DESTROYED
```

The physical village may remain as ruined or partially inhabited terrain. `DESTROYED` means that canonical Spree no longer functions as the authored civic polity.

The route must not become a generic Villager kill quota or require every house and workstation to be erased.

Other Villager settlements remain ordinary independent populations.

## 8. Villager Retaliation style provider architecture

Spree is the clearest expression of the V5 provider-driven civilization model.

Local people and institutions expose the political routes through concrete problems and services.

Required design rule:

```text
provider chain
-> named civic fact, service, obligation, weakness or dependency
-> exact terminal route prerequisite where relevant
```

The terminal choice must therefore be earned by the state of Spree's civic pillars. The civilization Questlog must not reduce the arc to an abstract three-button political selection disconnected from local content.

## 9. Source and implementation discipline

Franchise authority supports:

- Spree civic leadership using the title Mayor;
- local political organization rather than one universal government model;
- farms and food supply as strategic settlement concerns;
- trade and paid service as ordinary social/economic behavior.

Minecraft implementation must then select source-compatible Villager and Guard Villager mechanics to express those approved themes.

The next technical/objective pass should prefer native professions, trades, farming actions, raids, Guard Villager behaviors, workstation use or other installed mechanics only where they serve the approved political meaning.

Do not add a general Villager reputation system simply to connect them.

## 10. Presenter use

The Mayor enters the final presenter roster.

Additional food/agriculture, commerce/specialist or Guard Villager providers enter the roster only if their finalized chains give them direct Questlog speech.

All use the universal five-state presenter vocabulary:

```text
neutral
pleased
assertive
concerned
hostile
```

The Spree identity, Mayor role, civic-viability political spine and all three terminal route meanings are approved.
