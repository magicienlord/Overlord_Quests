# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Farming and Magical Automation Authored Blueprint

Status: V5 QUEST BLUEPRINT

Purpose: define the compact authored progression from an ordinary productive farm into an assisted estate farm using Crop Critters and a Hay Golem, without turning agriculture into a broad technology tree.

No final quest names or final dialogue are assigned here. Beat IDs are authoring references.

## 1. Campaign role

This arc is separate from Gluttony magic.

Farmer's Delight represents the mundane productive foundation. Crop Critters represent magical agricultural labor. Golem Overhaul contributes agricultural protection through the Hay Golem.

The arc proves that the Overlord can establish a functioning estate whose production is assisted by magical labor and protected by an appropriate agricultural construct.

It does not require combat golems, exhaustive crop catalogs, every Farmer's Delight meal, or every automation option in the pack.

## 2. FARM-010: Establish productive agriculture

Status: PLANNED

Prerequisites:

- central opening `CEN-010` complete;
- ordinary Overworld farming materials available.

Visible accomplishments:

- establish a real cultivated farm using ordinary crops, including at least one Farmer's Delight crop or production element;
- harvest meaningful produce from the established farm;
- prepare or store a useful food output from that production.

Purpose:

- prove that the estate has an ordinary agricultural foundation before magical labor is introduced.

Completion boundary:

- implementation should use a small representative set of stable crop/harvest/craft signals;
- no crop encyclopedia or acreage detector is required.

Unlocks:

- `FARM-020`.

## 3. FARM-020: Introduce Crop Critter labor

Status: PLANNED

Prerequisite:

- `FARM-010` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- obtain, create, recruit, or otherwise establish one appropriate Crop Critter through its native progression;
- place or use it in the agricultural context for which it is designed;
- demonstrate one real native labor action that contributes to the farm.

Purpose:

- establish magical labor as a practical extension of the farm rather than as a collectible creature checklist.

Implementation boundary:

- the exact Crop Critter type and exact labor proof must be selected from the installed source surface during translation;
- prefer the simplest reliable native action that visibly assists crop production;
- do not require every Critter variant.

Unlocks:

- `FARM-030`.

## 4. FARM-030: Establish agricultural protection

Status: PLANNED

Prerequisite:

- `FARM-020` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- create or establish a Hay Golem through Golem Overhaul's native progression;
- deploy it as the farm's agricultural protection / utility construct;
- demonstrate that it is functioning in the estate context.

Purpose:

- add protection and utility appropriate to an agricultural estate without opening a general combat-golem questline.

Boundary:

- other golems remain native play unless a separate approved arc later needs one;
- no combat-golem escalation is required for this arc.

Unlocks:

- `FARM-040`.

## 5. FARM-040: Functioning assisted estate

Status: PLANNED

Prerequisite:

- `FARM-030` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- demonstrate one complete assisted production cycle in which the established farm produces useful output while the approved Crop Critter labor and Hay Golem protection/utility are both present and functioning.

Purpose:

- close the arc on a functioning system rather than on possession of three disconnected mod items.

Completion state:

- record the assisted-estate farming arc complete.

Implementation boundary:

- Questlog should prove the component accomplishments and their continued availability without requiring fragile continuous simulation of an autonomous economy;
- if no single native event can prove the complete cycle, completion may be expressed as an AND of the already-authored durable component facts plus one final real harvest/production action at the estate.

## 6. Native remainder

The following remain native or sandbox play:

- maximizing crop yield;
- every Farmer's Delight crop and meal;
- every Crop Critter variant;
- every Golem Overhaul construct;
- combat-golem collection;
- large automatic farm engineering;
- storage, transport and processing optimization beyond what other authored arcs require.

This arc exists to establish a coherent estate-production capability, not to certify the entire farming mod stack.
