# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Ramblings Authority

Status: WORKING V5 AUTHORITY, STRICTLY LIMITED TO EXPLICIT OVERLORD APPROVAL

Date: 2026-09-16

Authority: subordinate to `docs/V5_00_OVERLORD_APPROVAL_GOVERNANCE.md`.

Purpose: record the approved role of Ramblings without selecting unapproved native triggers, speakers, quotas, or per-mod mappings.

## 1. Approved system role

`OVERLORD APPROVED`:

- Ramblings are sparse contextual character reactions.
- Ramblings are not quests.
- Ramblings are not reminder systems.
- Ramblings are not a second hidden completion track.
- Meaningful native achievements, discoveries, or milestones may be allocated to Ramblings instead of dedicated Questlog beats when the Overlord explicitly approves that allocation.
- Not every native advancement deserves a Rambling.
- Native progression that does not receive an authored V5 allocation remains native progression.

A technical source exposing an advancement, statistic, structure trigger, boss kill, item acquisition, or other event proves only that the event exists and may be detectable. It does not authorize a Rambling.

## 2. Exact trigger selection requires approval

The following are authored decisions and therefore require explicit Overlord approval:

- whether a specific native event receives a Rambling at all;
- whether it is required, optional, or excluded from Rambling treatment;
- the exact speaker or presenter;
- any knowledge prerequisite;
- any timing or retrospective-presentation rule that changes player experience;
- any quota, milestone count, or per-mod minimum;
- any replacement of a Questlog beat with a Rambling;
- any conversion of ambient or native content into a Rambling.

No author may infer these choices from thematic fit alone.

## 3. Established presenter ownership relevant to Ramblings

`OVERLORD APPROVED`:

- Gristle owns food-adjacent Ramblings.
- Presenter ownership is thematic and contributes to a varying pool of eligible quest-presenting NPCs.

Other exact Rambling speaker assignments remain governed by their explicit V5 decisions. Existing source affinity, provider identity, or technical origin does not automatically assign a speaker.

## 4. L_Ender's Cataclysm allocation

`OVERLORD APPROVED` at the general level:

L_Ender's Cataclysm may use meaningful native achievements as popup Ramblings to complement its broader Questlog coverage and deliver established REIGN context near the relevant discovery or victory.

This approval does not select the exact advancement list.

Therefore:

- the exact Cataclysm achievement-to-Rambling table remains `UNKNOWN / REQUIRES OVERLORD DECISION`;
- exact speaker ownership remains `UNKNOWN / REQUIRES OVERLORD DECISION` unless separately approved;
- an achievement with established technical existence is not automatically included;
- an achievement with no approved REIGN interpretation must not receive invented history.

Earlier assistant-created lists that marked particular Cataclysm structure or boss advancements as `Required`, `Preferred`, or otherwise selected are `SUPERSEDED / NOT APPROVED` until individually reviewed.

## 5. Other mod and campaign allocations

No exact Fathoms, NightWalker, Dragon, End, civilization, Church of Sin, Born in Chaos, magic, farming, equipment, creature, structure, or other native event receives a Rambling solely because an earlier assistant-authored V5 file listed it.

Those selections must be reviewed as authored decisions.

The general existence of sparse Ramblings does not authorize:

- one Rambling per mod;
- one Rambling per boss;
- one Rambling per structure;
- one Rambling per advancement tree;
- one Rambling for every unused native progression milestone.

## 6. Technical work allowed without a new authored decision

After a specific Rambling has been approved, technical investigation may determine without further campaign design:

- exact advancement or statistic identifier;
- exact source event or durable condition;
- whether the event can be detected reliably;
- persistence wiring for one-time display where one-time behavior has already been approved;
- compatibility hook location;
- validation and CI mechanics.

If several technically valid detectors would produce materially different player-facing behavior, that difference returns to the Overlord for decision.

## 7. Final V5 completeness requirement

Before V5 is considered complete, every Rambling that final campaign implementation is expected to contain must be explicitly enumerable with its approved authored fields.

At minimum, each final entry must have explicit authority for:

```text
rambling identity
campaign or thematic ownership
source event selected for presentation
speaker or presenter
knowledge prerequisites where applicable
player-facing purpose
repeat or one-time behavior if authored rather than purely technical
```

This requirement does not authorize the author to fill missing values.

Anything missing remains `UNKNOWN / REQUIRES OVERLORD DECISION` until the Overlord decides it.
