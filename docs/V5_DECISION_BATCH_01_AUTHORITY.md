## Decisions

### Q001 — Opening / Tower / Tower restoration proof model

Decision status: APPROVED

Question: For ordinary Tower rooms, should restoration complete when the required functional anchor is installed, with actual mastery/use left to that system's own questline?

Authoritative decision: Approve installation as the restoration proof. Do not require a second use-test unless that room's purpose specifically depends on activation.

### Q002 — Opening / Tower / Forge room composition

Decision status: APPROVED

Question: What exact functional installation should the Forge restoration quest require?

Authoritative decision: Require both the Hot Iron Smithing Anvil and Crucible so the room visibly supports forging and processing rather than being a one-block token installation.

### Q003 — Opening / Tower / Storage room composition

Decision status: APPROVED

Question: What exact Storage Drawers installation should prove the Storage Room is operational?

Authoritative decision: Require a Drawer Controller connected to at least one functional drawer bank. Exact drawer style/count is not progression-critical.

### Q004 — Opening / Tower / Armory composition

Decision status: APPROVED

Question: How should the approved 'multiple display families' rule be translated into the Armory restoration objective?

Authoritative decision: Require at least one armor-display installation and one weapon-display installation from distinct supported display families.

### Q005 — Opening / Tower / WayGates restoration

Decision status: APPROVED WITH CLARIFICATION

Question: What should the WayGates restoration quest prove beyond placing the Tower Waystone?

Authoritative decision: Tower WayGates restoration tracks only placement of the Tower Waystone. The intended acquisition context is that the player finds a Waystone in the wild and brings it back to the Tower, but finding/discovering it and travelling with it are not additional tracked restoration objectives.

### Q006 — Opening / Tower / Magical-room restoration rule

Decision status: APPROVED

Question: Should Alchemy, Theurgy, Gluttony, Spell Study, Eidolon and Biomancy restoration require only their primary functional workstation(s), with deeper use reserved for their own progression arcs?

Authoritative decision: Yes. Tower Restoration installs the room's core workstation(s); mastery quests prove actual system use later.

### Q103 — Cross-system / Sequence-break policy

Decision status: APPROVED WITH SCOPE NOTE

Question: What general rule should V5 use when the player completes an irreversible objective before its quest becomes active?

Authoritative decision: Recognize durable prior history for irreversible accomplishments such as boss kills, major advancements and one-time discoveries. For repeatable ordinary actions, require the action after quest activation unless repeating it would be impossible or nonsensical. This is defensive robustness, not a design target: the intended playthrough is solo from a fresh start, and normal campaign authoring should not assume deliberate sequence breaking.

### Q104 — Cross-system / Bound provider protection

Decision status: MODIFIED

Question: Should quest-critical bound civilization providers be protected from incidental death until the route explicitly permits their removal?

Authoritative decision: Quest-critical bound civilization providers remain protected from incidental death until an authored destructive route explicitly authorizes their removal. When such an NPC is killed through that destructive branch, spawn a Ghosts-mod ghost that uses the Villager Retaliation-style framework to berate the Overlord for dooming the civilization. Those ghosts remain protected like the original critical NPCs and have no later destructive branch. Approximately four to five permanent ghosts per civilization is acceptable.

### Q105 — Cross-system / Destructive-route killability

Decision status: APPROVED

Question: When should protected political authorities become valid destructive targets?

Authoritative decision: Only after the destructive inner route has been revealed and deliberately committed to, not merely because the player attacks them early.
