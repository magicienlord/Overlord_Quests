## 10. Destructive-route protection and aftermath

Quest-critical bound civilization providers and authorities are protected from incidental death until an authored destructive route permits their removal.

A protected political authority becomes killable only after the destructive inner route has been revealed and deliberately committed to.

When a quest-critical bound NPC is killed through an authorized destructive branch:

```text
spawn a Ghosts-mod ghost representing the dead quest-critical character
-> retain civilization/provider identity for aftermath dialogue
-> use a Villager Retaliation-style interaction framework to berate the Overlord for dooming the civilization
-> permanently protect the ghost
-> do not give the ghost another destructive branch
```

Roughly four to five permanent aftermath ghosts per civilization is acceptable where the authored destructive route naturally kills that many quest-critical characters.

Final ghost dialogue remains deferred with final dialogue writing.
