# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Compact Adventures Authored Blueprint

Status: V5 QUEST BLUEPRINT

Purpose: define the smaller authored Adventure arcs whose campaign roles and endpoints are already established strongly enough to write without reopening their basic assignment.

No final quest names or final dialogue are assigned here. Beat IDs are authoring references.

## 1. Shared rules

1. These arcs are optional unless a future V5 section explicitly states otherwise.
2. They may become available naturally after the central opening as their world and capability prerequisites permit.
3. Their completion does not contribute to a hidden campaign percentage and is not required for Bosses'Rise or the Ender Dragon.
4. Native exploration and combat remain native where a quest beat does not need to duplicate them.
5. Early legitimate discovery or completion must be reconciled where durable source state can prove it.
6. Church of Sin and Born in Chaos do not receive dedicated V5 questlines despite older assignment documents that predate the V5 decision ledger.

## 2. The Graveyard

Campaign role:

- compact supernatural investigation and confrontation;
- preserve ambiguity around the Corrupted Champion / Lich identity;
- distinguish undeath and ritual corruption from ordinary resurrection.

### ADV-GRAVE-010: Investigate the ruins

Visible accomplishment:

- discover and enter the relevant Graveyard ruin / dungeon site tied to the Corrupted Champion progression.

Purpose:

- establish the location as a deliberate supernatural problem rather than a generic graveyard structure.

Presentation:

- Mortis is preferred where a dead/undead specialist voice is appropriate;
- Gnarl may provide strategic framing if required.

### ADV-GRAVE-020: Recover the three Ominous Bone Staff fragments

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-GRAVE-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- recover all three source-owned Ominous Bone Staff fragments through the native Graveyard progression.

Purpose:

- make the player's dungeon investigation produce the specific means needed for the confrontation.

Completion:

- exact items and source signals pinned during technical translation.

### ADV-GRAVE-030: Prepare the confrontation

Prerequisite:

- all three fragments recovered.

Visible accomplishment:

- assemble / restore / activate the Ominous Bone Staff or the exact source-owned confrontation key derived from those fragments.

Purpose:

- turn the investigation into intentional preparation rather than a random boss encounter.

### ADV-GRAVE-040: Defeat the Corrupted Champion

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-GRAVE-030` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- confront and defeat the Corrupted Champion / Lich through its native boss encounter.

Completion state:

- Graveyard Adventure complete.

Lore boundary:

- V5 does not identify the Champion's original personal identity beyond what existing REIGN authority establishes;
- no broad new doctrine of undead creation is inferred from this one case.

## 3. The Lost Castle

Campaign role:

- finite expedition through one major authored castle;
- present an unknown predecessor civilization's ruins now occupied by later Illager squatters and treasure seekers;
- conclude the expedition without granting automatic territorial ownership.

### ADV-CASTLE-010: Discover the castle

Visible accomplishment:

- locate and enter the Lost Castle structure.

Purpose:

- establish the site as a major expedition target.

Presentation:

- Gnarl may recognize that the castle predates its present occupants without claiming to know the predecessor civilization when the evidence does not support that knowledge.

### ADV-CASTLE-020: Establish who occupies it now

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-CASTLE-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- penetrate the occupied parts of the castle deeply enough to establish that Illagers are later occupants / squatters rather than the original builders.

Purpose:

- separate current occupation from the site's unknown historical origin.

Implementation boundary:

- use one or more concrete structure-depth, encounter, or source-owned progression signals, not a generic kill quota.

### ADV-CASTLE-030: Reach the castle's principal interior objective

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-CASTLE-020` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- reach the principal treasure / central chamber / source-owned culmination area of the castle and secure the expedition's meaningful reward or evidence.

Purpose:

- give the large structure a finite exploration climax.

Boundary:

- exact chamber and reward must be selected from the installed Lost Castle source rather than invented.

### ADV-CASTLE-040: Conclude the expedition

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-CASTLE-030` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- leave the central expedition with the principal reward/evidence secured and the immediate hostile occupation problem resolved as required by the source structure.

Completion state:

- Lost Castle Adventure complete.

Explicit non-consequence:

- completion does not automatically record the castle as Overlord territory;
- completion does not identify the predecessor civilization unless later evidence actually establishes it.

## 4. Oddities / Queen of Orchid

Campaign role:

- compact nature-spirit / magical-predator encounter;
- preserve the Orchid Shrine as local worship or containment rather than evidence of a new civilization.

Established meaning:

- the Queen of Orchid is an ancient Overworld nature-spirit / magical predator;
- the entity and shrine are independent of the Cataclysm and major imperial history unless later evidence says otherwise.

### ADV-ORCHID-010: Discover the Orchid Shrine

Visible accomplishment:

- locate and enter the source-owned Orchid Shrine.

Purpose:

- establish the local cultic/containment setting around the Queen.

### ADV-ORCHID-020: Activate the Orchid Altar

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-ORCHID-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- complete the source-owned altar preparation / activation required to deliberately invoke the Queen of Orchid encounter.

Purpose:

- make the boss confrontation an intentional culmination of the shrine rather than a random spawn.

### ADV-ORCHID-030: Defeat the Queen of Orchid

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-ORCHID-020` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- defeat the Queen of Orchid and obtain the encounter's meaningful source-owned reward where one exists.

Completion state:

- Orchid Adventure complete.

Lore boundary:

- the shrine does not create a new civilization or global religion category.

## 5. Pet Cemetery / Mortis

Campaign role:

- small conditional arc that appears only after one of the Overlord's tamed pets dies;
- introduce the source-supported resurrection process without implying routine resurrection of ordinary sapient beings.

Principal presenter:

- Mortis.

### ADV-PET-010: Recover the dead pet's link

Trigger:

- a supported tamed pet owned by the player dies and the mod generates that pet's Pet Collar.

Visible accomplishment:

- recover the generated Pet Collar belonging to that specific dead pet.

Purpose:

- begin the arc only from a real loss and bind the resurrection sequence to that individual pet.

### ADV-PET-020: Return the pet as undead

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-PET-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- use the specific collar with the source-supported charged Respawn Anchor process;
- return that same pet in its native zombie-pet state.

Purpose:

- demonstrate that the source system first restores the pet imperfectly rather than erasing death without consequence.

### ADV-PET-030: Restore living form

Prerequisite:

- the bound pet exists in its zombie-pet state.

Visible accomplishment:

- cure that same returned zombie pet back to living form.

Authoritative completion signal:

- source-owned `cured_zombie_pet` progression signal or the exact equivalent exposed by the installed build.

Completion state:

- Mortis pet-resurrection arc complete.

Native remainder:

- the separate skeleton-pet path remains optional native play or sparse Mortis commentary;
- it is not required for the authored resurrection arc.

## 6. Quaver and the Tower band

Campaign role:

- small Dark Tower personnel arc;
- restore a proper evil court atmosphere through Quaver's effort to organize live music;
- deliberately reference Overlord II court life without making music a Tower Restoration gate.

Principal presenter/provider:

- Quaver.

### TOWER-QUAVER-010: Restore the means of performance

Prerequisites:

- central opening complete;
- Dark Tower accessible as normal.

Visible accomplishment:

- acquire / craft the Immersive Melodies instruments or performance equipment needed to begin rebuilding a Tower band.

Purpose:

- support Quaver's character goal, not teach every instrument recipe.

Boundary:

- exact minimum instrument set remains a later content choice and must be based on the final intended band composition.

### TOWER-QUAVER-020: Assemble the band

Prerequisite:

- `TOWER-QUAVER-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- recruit, assign, or otherwise establish the performers required for the intended Tower band.

Purpose:

- turn the arc into personnel restoration rather than item collection.

Presentation rule:

- any speaking recruited characters added by this arc enter the final V5 presenter-roster review if they receive authored dialogue.

Current V5 boundary:

- exact performer count and identities are not yet established by current authority and must not be invented during technical implementation.

### TOWER-QUAVER-030: Restore court performance

Prerequisite:

- intended band assembled.

Visible accomplishment:

- complete one deliberate band performance in the Dark Tower using the assembled personnel and Immersive Melodies system.

Purpose:

- close the arc on restored court life rather than equipment ownership.

Completion state:

- Quaver Tower-personnel arc complete.

Consequence:

- improved court atmosphere / personnel presence;
- no effect on formal initial Tower Restoration readiness.

### Born in Chaos

V5 allocation:

- no dedicated questline;
- modern dangerous ecology;
- selected bosses or meaningful achievements may receive sparse Ramblings;
- individual content may be absorbed into another authored arc only when it genuinely serves that arc.

### General Mowzie's Mobs progression

V5 allocation:

- no generic Mowzie boss checklist;
- Umvuthi belongs to the Umvuthana civilization;
- the Conjurer belongs in Illager / Heaven's Peak material;
- Frostmaw, Wroughtnaut, Sculptor and other major encounters remain standalone Adventure/native/Rambling material unless a later authored need specifically adopts them.

## 8. Current V5 decision boundary

The compact arcs above expose one unresolved authored choice:

- Quaver's intended final band composition, including the number and identities of recurring performers, is not established yet.

This choice affects `TOWER-QUAVER-010`, `TOWER-QUAVER-020`, the final speaking-character roster, and later presenter PNG production. It should be fixed as campaign content before implementation decides performer slots arbitrarily.
