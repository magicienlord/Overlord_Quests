# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Ribbit Civilization Blueprint

Status: WORKING V5 CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY

Date: 2026-09-16

Authority relationship: this document is a focused civilization blueprint under `docs/V5_CAMPAIGN_SYSTEM_AUTHORITY.md` and `docs/V5_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM_AUTHORITY.md`. It records explicit V5 decisions approved by the Overlord and source-backed mechanics from Ribbits 3.0.5.

## 1. Canonical anchor

The canonical Ribbit polity is one runtime-selected Ribbit Village.

The village is one local community and does not represent every Ribbit settlement.

The deliberate anchor-start action is Questlog provider interaction with a native Gardener inside an eligible Ribbit Village. That individual becomes the local Elder for the selected anchor.

Required pattern:

```text
find eligible Ribbit Village
-> deliberately interact with local Gardener through Questlog provider flow
-> verify no Ribbit anchor is already locked
-> bind village as canonical Ribbit anchor
-> bind interacted Gardener as Elder
-> bind or spawn required Fisherman, Merchant and Sorcerer providers
-> expose Ribbit civilization Questlog
```

The Elder title is local and authored. Ordinary Gardeners elsewhere remain ordinary Gardeners.

## 2. Political spine

The approved Ribbit model deliberately has no fabricated crisis.

Ribbits remain peaceful, cozy, musical, cheerful and slightly silly. Their village already functions through agriculture, fishing, trade, magical support and communal music.

The political question is therefore the Overlord's treatment of a harmless society that presents no serious threat.

The campaign must not manufacture a war, famine, military emergency or grim hidden conspiracy merely to create stakes.

The dark comedy comes from the contrast between the village's innocence and the Overlord's ability to help it, bind it into service, or destroy it anyway.

## 3. Required recurring cast

### 3.1 Gardener-Elder

Source role:

```text
Gardener
```

Authored local role:

```text
Elder of the canonical Ribbit Village
```

Purpose:

- civilization starter;
- represents the village's agricultural and civic continuity;
- final authority for NEUTRAL and SUBJUGATED resolution.

### 3.2 Fisherman

Purpose:

- represents food gathering and local supply;
- supports ordinary village-life content;
- may become a specific supply obligation in SUBJUGATED.

### 3.3 Merchant

Purpose:

- represents the village's amethyst-centered exchange economy;
- supports ordinary trade content;
- may become a tribute or commerce obligation in SUBJUGATED.

### 3.4 Sorcerer

Purpose:

- represents the village's magical support function;
- source AI already provides buff behavior;
- may become a magical-service obligation in SUBJUGATED.

Music remains an important characterization surface and may support provider scenes, objectives or Ramblings where meaningful. It is not automatically a separate political pillar.

## 4. NEUTRAL route

The independent route participates in ordinary Ribbit life and improves the village without claiming it.

Approved structure:

```text
anchor locked
-> complete meaningful Gardener content
-> complete meaningful Fisherman content
-> complete meaningful Merchant content
-> complete meaningful Sorcerer or village-magic content
-> allow musical and communal characterization to appear naturally
-> demonstrate that the village can continue its peaceful life independently
-> final Elder resolution
-> deliberately leave the village autonomous
-> NEUTRAL
```

The route is not a friendship meter. The final independence settlement is explicit.

## 5. SUBJUGATED route

Subjugation converts innocent local favors and services into explicit political obligations.

Approved principal pillars:

```text
Merchant obligation
Fisherman obligation
Sorcerer obligation
```

The route resolves as:

```text
anchor locked
-> complete the three specific inner provider chains
-> Merchant commits tribute, commerce or supply obligation
-> Fisherman commits food or resource supply obligation
-> Sorcerer commits magical service obligation
-> obligations are presented to the Gardener-Elder as the village's new political reality
-> Elder knowingly accepts that the canonical village now serves beneath the Overlord
-> SUBJUGATED
```

The Ribbits understand that they have been subjugated. V5 must not make the outcome depend on deceiving them into failing to understand their political status.

Their personality and presentation may remain cheerful, simple or awkward even while acknowledging the result.

No hidden reputation score or generic sidequest count is permitted.

## 6. DESTROYED route

The destructive route is intentionally cruel because the Ribbits offered no serious provocation.

Approved meaning:

```text
player deliberately chooses destruction despite peaceful relations
-> authored protection lifts only as required by the destructive route
-> remove the Elder and the civilization-specific indispensable local functions selected by the detailed objective pass
-> village ceases to function as the canonical polity
-> DESTROYED
```

The route follows the universal V5 `DESTROYED` rule. It does not require every building to be erased and must not become a generic Ribbit kill quota.

Ribbits and other villages remain extant elsewhere.

## 7. Technical objective rule

Ribbits 3.0.5 has no useful accomplishment advancement ladder. Its packaged advancements are recipe unlocks.

Native systems nevertheless provide source-valid actions through:

- crop care;
- fishing behavior;
- Sorcerer buffs;
- MerchantOffer trade;
- music and player-facing Maraca content.

Where V5 requires proof of a specific Ribbit transaction or interaction, the later implementation may use a narrow filtered bridge rather than the broad vanilla trade statistic.

Do not create a parallel Ribbit economy or artificial reputation meter.
