Technical consequence:

- the canonical anchor can visibly contain forge, mine, storage, farm, tower and residential functions;
- these are worldgen structures, not new political offices;
- V5 should not invent Piglin blacksmith, farmer, miner or other profession NPC classes merely because the village contains those facilities.

## 3. Native Piglin systems remain vanilla-owned

The civilization arc can rely on vanilla Piglin behavior and state, including:

- gold-armor tolerance for ordinary Piglins;
- Piglin Brute hostility;
- gold bartering;
- anger over protected containers;
- anger over mining local gold or gold-related blocks;
- ordinary Piglin social response to player aggression.

These systems are implementation ingredients. They do not automatically write civilization disposition.

## 4. Existing REIGN authority

Already-established authority remains:

- the canonical anchor is one runtime-selected Nether Village;
- a Piglin Brute receives the authored local title `Chieftain` and is the civilization starter;
- Piglins descend from an ancient Minion and pig union;
- Gnarl is embarrassed by that true ancestry;
- Piglins expanded during the Silence as Minion numbers and territorial dominance collapsed;
- the selected Nether Village is a Piglin-built polity, not abandoned Minion infrastructure;
- Piglins and Minions recognize one another as unusually close Netherworld cousin-peoples;
- other Piglins, Brutes, Bastions and settlements remain native and independent;
- subjugation must make the canonical village safe for the Overlord regardless of gold armor and must permit local container access and local gold extraction without native retaliation;
- `DESTROYED` means the selected anchor ceases to function as a polity, not that every village block must be erased.

## 5. Remaining V5 gap

The source and existing canon do not yet determine the exact political pressure that distinguishes:

```text
independent coexistence -> NEUTRAL
submission to the returning Master -> SUBJUGATED
deliberate destruction -> DESTROYED
```

The village facilities, vanilla gold economy, territorial history and Minion kinship provide strong ingredients, but the political meaning must be approved before they are turned into route objectives.
