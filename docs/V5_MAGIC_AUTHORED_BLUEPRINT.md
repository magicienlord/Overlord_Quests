# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Magic Authored Blueprint

Status: V5 QUEST BLUEPRINT

Purpose: define the authored quest structures for the principal magical disciplines without turning Questlog into a collection of mod manuals.

No final quest names or final dialogue are assigned here. Beat IDs are authoring references.

## 1. Shared magic rules

1. Tower room restoration only establishes the relevant workspace. It does not complete or replace mastery of the discipline.
2. Magic arcs become available after their required workspace and ordinary material prerequisites are available. They do not gate the central campaign unless explicitly stated elsewhere.
3. Each quest represents a meaningful increase in understanding or capability.
4. Native recipes, every workstation, every spell, every ritual, every serum, and every material tier are not automatically Questlog content.
5. Unused meaningful native advancements may support sparse NPC Ramblings instead of additional quests.
6. Cross-system ingredients may appear where the validated modpack already supports them, but each discipline keeps its established domain.
7. The Overlord can master all disciplines. A recorded Good/Evil inclination is not a morality score and does not prevent later native use of the opposite path.
8. Where a final mastery beat uses an OR condition, only the first qualifying route writes the remembered inclination. Later completion of the opposite native route does not reopen the quest or overwrite that first result.

## 2. Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks

Campaign role:

- teach active mana manipulation as a practical magical discipline;
- move from possessing magic to deliberately shaping and expressing it;
- end with a remembered first demonstrated mana inclination rather than exhaustive school completion.

The installed progression provides spellbooks, scrolls, mana, inks, Inscription Table, Scroll Forge, Arcane Anvil, runes, spellbook upgrades and multiple spell schools. V5 selects only the progression necessary to prove authorship and mastery.

### MAG-IRON-010: Put active magic in hand

Prerequisites:

- Spell Study Tower function operational.

Visible accomplishments:

- obtain or craft a usable spellbook;
- equip the spellbook;
- successfully cast a spell from it.

Purpose:

- distinguish active spellcasting from merely finding magical loot;
- establish that the Overlord can personally manipulate mana.

Completion:

- source-backed item/equip/use or casting observation during implementation.

### MAG-IRON-020: Prepare a spell expression

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-IRON-010` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- obtain the material substrate required for authored spell preparation, including appropriate ink and scroll resources;
- use the relevant Iron's inscription / scrollmaking workflow to produce or intentionally configure a spell-bearing scroll.

Purpose:

- move from consuming found magic to deliberately preparing magical expression.

Boundary:

- V5 does not require every ink tier or every scroll rarity.

### MAG-IRON-030: Shape the spell

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-IRON-020` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- use the established spellmaking / inscription system to create, inscribe, or improve a spell setup beyond the introductory state, then cast from that authored setup.

Purpose:

- prove that the Overlord can shape a spell configuration rather than merely wield an unchanged found spell.

Implementation boundary:

- exact minimum tier and exact workstation sequence should be selected from the installed 3.16.3 progression so this remains one mastery beat rather than a workstation checklist.

### MAG-IRON-040: Demonstrate advanced control

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-IRON-030` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- successfully cast a deliberately prepared higher-capability spell from an appropriately developed spellbook.

Purpose:

- prove sustained mana control at a level meaningfully beyond the introductory spell.

Boundary:

- this does not require a maximum-tier spellbook, every school, every rune, or every upgrade orb.

### MAG-IRON-050: First mana inclination

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-IRON-040` complete.

Structure:

- one Questlog beat using `OrObjective`;
- Good Mana proof OR Evil Mana proof completes the beat;
- whichever qualifying proof completes first writes the remembered Iron's inclination.

Good Mana proof:

- demonstrate meaningful Holy magic, because Holy is explicitly established as an expression of Good Mana.

Evil Mana proof:

- demonstrate meaningful Blood magic, which is the V5-authorized Iron's proof of Evil Mana mastery.

Completion state:

- `GOOD_FIRST` or `EVIL_FIRST` style remembered fact, exact implementation key later;
- no morality score;
- no permanent school lock.

After completion:

- opposite-path native mastery remains available;
- later meaningful opposite mastery may produce one optional Rambling, but does not reopen this quest.

## 3. Farmer's Spell / Gluttony

- demonstrate the transition from mundane prepared cuisine into genuine Gluttony magic;
- preserve Farmer's Delight as mundane cuisine and Farmer's Spell as magical cuisine.

The installed Farmer's Spell already directly bridges Farmer's Delight food systems with Iron's Arcane materials through the Alchemist Pot and magical recipes.

### MAG-GLUT-010: Prepare the mundane base

- Gluttony Tower function operational;
- ordinary Farmer's Delight access.

Visible accomplishment:

- prepare one substantial mundane Farmer's Delight meal or culinary base appropriate for later magical treatment.

Purpose:

- establish that Gluttony begins with actual cuisine rather than replacing cooking with a magic table.

Boundary:

- exact meal should be selected for stable recipe support and thematic fit during implementation;
- no general Farmer's Delight recipe checklist.

### MAG-GLUT-020: Build the magical bridge

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-GLUT-010` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- construct or establish the Alchemist Pot;
- complete one successful Arcane Cooking recipe that visibly combines mundane culinary components with magical resources.

Purpose:

- demonstrate the discipline's defining bridge between prepared food and mana.

### MAG-GLUT-030: Demonstrate Gluttony magic

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-GLUT-020` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- produce a meaningful Gluttony magical food or beverage through the established system;
- consume or otherwise use the result so its magical function is demonstrated rather than merely collected.

Purpose:

- close the compact arc with actual magical cuisine in use.

Boundary:

- exact culmination recipe should be chosen from the repaired, validated Farmer's Spell recipe surface;
- broken or uncertain legacy recipes must not be used merely because their assets exist.

## 4. Theurgy

Campaign role:

- teach occult material science through extraction, informational pattern, reconstruction, and controlled transformation;
- demonstrate why Theurgy can reproduce matter without turning the questline into every-machine certification.

OVERLORD REIGN interpretation:

- Salt is material body/substrate;
- Sulfur is informational material pattern;
- Mercury is processed material energy;
- Theurgic Sulfur is not a personal soul.

### MAG-THEURGY-010: Extract a material principle

Prerequisites:

- Theurgy Tower function operational.

Visible accomplishment:

- take a normal source material through the native early processing chain far enough to produce its material-specific Alchemical Sulfur or equivalent informational principle.

Purpose:

- teach that the discipline first learns what matter is before reproducing it.

### MAG-THEURGY-020: Produce usable material energy

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-THEURGY-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- produce and use the relevant Mercury / Mercury Flux side of the Theurgy process in one real transformation.

Purpose:

- establish the energy component required to enact a stored material pattern.

Boundary:

- exact apparatus chain is selected from the installed progression, not expanded into one quest per machine.

### MAG-THEURGY-030: Reproduce matter

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-THEURGY-020` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- reform or replicate a material from its extracted Theurgic pattern through the native reformation system.

Purpose:

- prove genuine material reproduction.

Constraint:

- use a material whose replication is already valid in the approved progression boundaries;
- do not introduce a progression-key material merely to make this quest impressive.

### MAG-THEURGY-040: Transform material class

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-THEURGY-030` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- perform a meaningful native transmutation that changes one material state or category into another rather than simply reproducing the original sample.

Purpose:

- demonstrate that the Overlord understands the broader occult laws of matter, not only duplication.

Completion:

- Theurgy mastery arc complete.

Native remainder:

- extensive replication catalogs, every Sulfur family, all divination rods, every catalyst tier, and large-scale resource automation remain native play.

## 5. Ars Elixirum

Campaign role:

- teach magical pharmacology through property discovery, deliberate formulation, and controlled mastery;
- emphasize understanding of effects and formulation rather than generic potion collection.

The validated B4 build preserves ingredient discovery, Honey Solvent extraction, Glass Cauldron formulation, trait discovery, Mastery increase and potion persistence.

### MAG-ELIX-010: Discover ingredient properties

Prerequisites:

- Alchemy workspace operational.

Visible accomplishments:

- use the native discovery/extraction system, including Honey Solvent where required, to reveal meaningful alchemical properties of selected ingredients.

Purpose:

- establish that ingredients are understood through pharmacological properties rather than arbitrary recipe memorization.

Boundary:

- a small representative set is sufficient;
- no requirement to catalogue the entire modpack's generated ingredient registry.

### MAG-ELIX-020: Formulate an elixir

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-ELIX-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- deliberately combine discovered properties in the Glass Cauldron and produce a successful saved elixir with a useful effect.

Purpose:

- prove controlled formulation from known properties.

### MAG-ELIX-030: Increase and apply mastery

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-ELIX-020` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- increase the player's native Ars Elixirum Mastery through successful controlled formulation;
- create a second, deliberately more advanced formulation that uses that improved understanding or a meaningful trait modifier;
- use the result.

Purpose:

- close the compact arc by demonstrating progression in personal pharmacological competence.

Native remainder:

- exhaustive Essence discovery, every Aspect, arbitrary potion experimentation, and full ingredient collection remain native.

- teach ritual occultism, soul handling, prepared rites and binding;
- end with a remembered first Sacred/Good versus Wicked/Evil ritual inclination.

Upstream deity language is not imported literally. Light/dark ritual forces are interpreted through Good Mana and Evil Mana.

- establish Eidolon as a learned ritual discipline rather than spontaneous spellcasting.

### MAG-EID-020: Prepare ritual matter

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-EID-010` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- establish and use the Crucible or equivalent core preparation system to create a meaningful ritual reagent or occult material.

Purpose:

- demonstrate deliberate ritual preparation.

### MAG-EID-030: Work with souls and rites

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-EID-020` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- obtain or produce a Soul Shard through native Eidolon progression;
- establish the Brazier / chant ritual capability;
- successfully complete one meaningful rite involving prepared occult or soul material.

Purpose:

### MAG-EID-040: Demonstrate binding or transformation

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-EID-030` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- complete one advanced native rite that demonstrates binding, transformation, consecration, desecration, or ritual necromancy beyond the introductory Brazier use.

Purpose:

### MAG-EID-050: First ritual inclination

Prerequisite:

- `MAG-EID-040` complete.

Structure:

- one Questlog beat using `OrObjective`;
- Sacred path proof OR Wicked path proof completes the beat;
- whichever qualifying path completes first writes the remembered Eidolon inclination.

Sacred proof:

- reach the native Sacred path culmination represented by the Holy Symbol or equivalent source-owned Sacred progression signal.

Wicked proof:

- reach the native Wicked path culmination represented by the Unholy Symbol or equivalent source-owned Wicked progression signal.

Completion state:

- remember Sacred/Good-first or Wicked/Evil-first inclination;
- no morality score;
- no permanent block against the other path.

After completion:

- opposite native mastery remains available;
- later completion of the opposite path may produce a single optional Rambling but does not reopen or rewrite the quest.

## 8. Cross-discipline boundaries

The following relationships are permitted but must not collapse quest identities:

- Farmer's Delight provides mundane food foundations to Gluttony and organic feedstock to Biomancy;
- Farmer's Spell legitimately consumes Iron's magical resources;
- selected Theurgy materials may support other apparatus where the validated progression already permits it;
- Ars Elixirum may use curated ingredients from the other disciplines without taking ownership of their progression;
- Eidolon soul-derived components may support later Blood-magic content without making Blood and Eidolon the same discipline;
- Biomancy may consume selected Ars Elixirum reagents where validated, but serums remain Biomancy products.

A cross-discipline item should appear in a quest only when it contributes to the authored mastery being demonstrated.

## 9. Current V5 decision boundary

No player-facing magic decision remains unresolved in this blueprint.

The Iron's closer is now fixed as:

- Good Mana proof through meaningful Holy magic;
- Evil Mana proof through meaningful Blood magic.

Exact registry IDs, exact spell choices within those approved schools, recipe choices, item tiers and objective detector details remain technical translation work unless source inspection exposes a new player-facing choice.
