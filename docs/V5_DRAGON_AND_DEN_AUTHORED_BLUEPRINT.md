# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Dragon Mastery and Dragon Den Authored Blueprint

Status: V5 QUEST BLUEPRINT

Purpose: translate the approved Ice & Fire dragon authority into the exact authored campaign-beat structure while preserving the separation between personal dragon mastery and later Dark Tower Dragon Den development.

No final quest names or final dialogue are assigned here. Beat IDs are authoring references.

## 1. Campaign allocation

Ice & Fire dragon content is split deliberately:

```text
dragon research and mastery -> dedicated Adventure progression
Dragon Den / Dragon Forge -> later Dark Tower development
Ashlord -> Bosses'Rise
Myrmex -> Civilization
miscellaneous Ice & Fire creatures -> native / Adventure / Rambling as appropriate
```

The Dragon Den is not part of the fourteen-function initial Tower readiness gate.

The four-step mastery line does not require Dragonsteel.

## 2. Dragon mastery sequence

### DRAGON-010: Establish dragon knowledge

Availability:

- available naturally after the central opening once Ice & Fire content can be encountered.

Visible accomplishments:

- obtain access to the Ice & Fire Bestiary / source-owned dragon research system;
- unlock or study enough dragon material to establish practical knowledge of wild dragons, their maturity, and their recoverable materials.

Purpose:

- begin with deliberate research rather than a random dragon kill;
- give later age, sex, egg and rearing objectives an in-world knowledge basis.

Boundary:

- no Bestiary completion requirement;
- exact Bestiary page threshold is technical translation work chosen to prove meaningful dragon study without turning the arc into a book checklist.

### DRAGON-020: Defeat a wild adult

Prerequisite:

- `DRAGON-010` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- locate and kill a wild adult dragon;
- harvest meaningful source-owned dragon materials from that same adult encounter.

Purpose:

- move from scholarship into proven ability against a mature dragon;
- establish access to real dragon remains rather than found loot alone.

Authority constraint:

- the broad Ice & Fire dragon-kill advancement is insufficient by itself if it cannot prove adulthood;
- the final implementation must preserve the authored adult requirement through the narrowest source-aware signal available.

Boundary:

- no specific element is mandatory at V5 level unless final source constraints require one;
- Ashlord does not satisfy this beat merely because he is a dragon-themed Bosses'Rise target.

### DRAGON-030: Secure an ancient female's egg

Prerequisite:

- `DRAGON-020` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- locate or defeat a female Stage 4 or Stage 5 dragon in the source-owned maturity model;
- obtain a dragon egg through the intended ancient-female context.

Purpose:

- establish the transition from hunting dragons to founding an owned dragon lineage;
- make the egg a consequence of engaging the correct mature creature rather than arbitrary later possession.

Authority constraints:

- Stage 4 or Stage 5 qualifies;
- Stage 5 is not mandatory solely for difficulty inflation;
- possession of an egg from an unrelated acquisition route is not automatically equivalent if the final source permits one.

### DRAGON-040: Raise and ride the owned dragon

Prerequisite:

- the egg secured through `DRAGON-030`.

Visible accomplishments:

- hatch that acquired egg;
- establish ownership of the resulting dragon;
- raise it to Stage 3 or later;
- ride it successfully.

Purpose:

- close mastery by proving control of the entire hunt-to-rearing progression;
- establish a living dragon asset that can later support the Dark Tower Dragon Den.

Explicit correction:

- no saddle is required;
- no armor requirement is added;
- the exact beta 5 source makes owned dragons rideable after Stage 2.

Completion state:

- dedicated dragon mastery Adventure complete;
- Dragon Den development may now become available as a separate Tower project;
- no Dragonsteel requirement has been imposed on mastery.

## 3. Dark Tower Dragon Den development

Campaign role:

- later independent Tower development;
- turn the Overlord's living dragon mastery into functioning dragon-breath industry;
- establish why a dedicated Den belongs in the Tower despite being excluded from initial restoration readiness.

Normal prerequisite:

- `DRAGON-040` complete;
- Dark Tower otherwise operational enough to support later development.

### TOWER-DRAGON-010: Establish the Den and Forge

Visible accomplishments:

- install the source-valid Dragon Forge multiblock / functional forge components appropriate to one owned dragon type;
- establish the Den area as a deliberate Tower facility capable of bringing the owned dragon into functional relation with the Forge.

Purpose:

- make the Dragon Den a functional Tower development rather than decorative stabling;
- establish the source-owned fact that the Forge requires dragon breath.

Boundary:

- V5 does not require all elemental Dragon Forge variants;
- exact room geometry remains world/build design, not Questlog progression;
- no fixed Tower coordinates or bounding-box quest logic are introduced.

### TOWER-DRAGON-020: Fire the Forge with living dragon breath

Prerequisite:

- `TOWER-DRAGON-010` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- use the owned dragon's correct breath to operate the installed Dragon Forge successfully;
- complete one real source-owned Dragon Forge production cycle.

Preferred functional proof:

- produce the first valid Dragonsteel result from the completed Forge where the exact pinned source progression supports that as the canonical Forge output.

Purpose:

- prove that the Den and Forge are actually operational;
- close the Tower-development arc on the source mechanic that justifies the room's existence.

Important separation:

- Dragonsteel here proves Dragon Den / Forge function;
- Dragonsteel remains unnecessary for `DRAGON-040` and therefore does not retroactively become part of dragon mastery.

Completion state:

- Dragon Den Tower development complete;
- the Forge remains native production infrastructure thereafter.

## 4. Compatibility add-on allocation

Ice and Fire Delight:

- no standalone questline;
- selected dragon-derived food may appear in Farming / cuisine / Gluttony only when it contributes to those authored arcs.

Ice and Fire: Spellbooks:

- no standalone questline;
- selected spell or equipment content may appear in Iron's or Dragon Den progression only when it materially supports the authored purpose;
- item names do not establish a Dragon Priest institution or religion.

## 5. Rambling allocation

Sparse reactions may acknowledge:

- first encounter with a notable non-quest dragon variant;
- unusually ancient Stage 5 encounters beyond the required arc;
- meaningful Dragon Forge achievements after the Den is already operational;
- notable Ice & Fire creatures not assigned a dedicated progression;
- significant compatible spell or cuisine milestones.

Do not turn leftover Ice & Fire achievements into a second checklist.

## 6. Technical boundary

Implementation must preserve source truth for:

- dragon age / stage;
- wild versus owned state;
- sex where required for egg context;
- egg lineage / acquisition context where technically possible;
- hatch ownership;
- Stage 3+ rideability;
- Dragon Forge multiblock validity;
- correct breath interaction;
- successful Forge output.

Exact registry IDs, event hooks and objective detectors are later technical translation work.

The V5 blueprint does not authorize production changes yet.
