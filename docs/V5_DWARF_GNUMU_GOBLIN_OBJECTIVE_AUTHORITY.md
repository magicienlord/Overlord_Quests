## 1. Universal DESTROYED interpretation

`DESTROYED` means that the selected canonical civilization anchor has irreversibly ceased to exist as a functioning polity.

It does not require the later implementation to prove that every settlement block has been physically demolished.

Required interpretation:

```text
complete the civilization-specific destructive route
-> deliberately remove the anchor's specifically authored leadership, indispensable authority, or other required functional pillars
-> complete any source-appropriate sabotage, demolition, or combat objectives selected for that civilization
-> anchor can no longer function as the authored polity
-> DESTROYED
```

Consequences:

- terminal disposition becomes permanent for the canonical anchor;
- civilization provider and service pools that depended on the destroyed polity close or transition to authored aftermath behavior;
- surviving structures may remain in the world as abandoned, damaged, looted, or ruined remnants;
- other settlements and members of the same civilization remain unaffected unless separately authored;
- physical burning, explosives, mining, or block demolition may be used where they provide good source-backed objectives, but no universal block-by-block destruction detector is required.

This rule supersedes any interpretation that `DESTROYED` requires total architectural erasure.

## 2. Dwarven Record Keeper

The approved historical provider is an otherwise professionless native Dwarf assigned the authored local title:

```text
Record Keeper
```

This is not a new Dwarven profession and does not imply that professionless Dwarves generally maintain archives.

The Record Keeper is the one already-established designated Dwarf who retains the surviving serious records of the Dwarven glory days.

Its route purpose is to expose the historical-legitimacy side of the successor hold.

The installed Dwarven Forge build contains no native historian, archivist profession, ancient-record item, or archival advancement. V5 therefore must not fabricate a source-native record object merely to make the provider mechanical.

The chain should combine:

- provider testimony;
- surviving Golden Hills institutional memory;
- concrete material continuity that the modern hold can actually demonstrate, including Dwarven metalworking, rune craft, mining, forge infrastructure, or other approved source-backed proof.

The Record Keeper's chain is the principal reveal path for the NEUTRAL route.

The previously approved Dwarven route allocation is binding:

```text
Record Keeper / history -> reveals NEUTRAL path
Dwarven Forger / forge viability -> reveals SUBJUGATED leverage
Dwarven Warrior / defensive and command vulnerabilities -> reveals DESTROYED path
```

The Forge-Thane remains the final political ruler for NEUTRAL and SUBJUGATED resolution.

The optional Dwarf-Kobold rivalry may modify leverage but remains nonmandatory.

## 3. Gnumu ancestry evidence chain

Present-day Gnumus possess remnants of genuine ancestral Vintage technology but no longer fully understand or reproduce the knowledge at its former level.

This is approved V5 canon for the regional Gnumu civilization.

The common ancestry investigation uses the exact native antiquity surface:

```text
obtain Vintage Metal
-> native Antiquity Shard milestone establishes genuine old Gnumu material
-> reconstruct or obtain a Gnumus Totem using the native Vintage Metal connection
-> present or investigate the old material and Totem through the civilization chain
-> Gnarl applies his surviving Mellow Hills historical knowledge
-> Gnarl identifies the deeper continuity with the regional Halflings altered by Gluttony magic
-> establish the already-approved Halfling / Gluttony ancestry truth
-> choose ANCESTRY_REVEALED or ANCESTRY_WITHHELD
```

The artifacts do not magically explain their own ancestry. Gnarl supplies the historical interpretation.

The local Gnumus still begin without this knowledge.

The ancestry decision remains independent of terminal political disposition.

Later Vintage reconstruction stays available for political and economic provider content rather than being exhausted by the ancestry chain. Appropriate later native progression includes Vintage Alloy, Vintage upgrades, and the Vintage Universal Tool where those actions support an approved route.

## 4. Goblin provider allocation

The approved provider split is:

### 4.1 NEUTRAL

Principal providers:

```text
Main Merchant
Camp Bartender
```

Purpose:

- demonstrate that ordinary trade and camp leisure can remain profitable under peaceful coexistence;
- establish concrete mutually beneficial relationships without creating a friendship meter;
- reveal the explicit independent-trade and nonaggression terminal route.

Native signals such as `goblins_tyranny:merchant_success` and `goblins_tyranny:liquor_success` may be used where they express the authored provider objectives.

### 4.2 SUBJUGATED

Principal provider pillars:

```text
Camp Engineer
Camp Blacksmith
Main Merchant
```

The source-native Blacksmith is an approved recurring provider for this route.

These three pillars represent:

- technology and engineering;
- arms and material production;
- commerce and access to profitable exchange.

Each chain must end in an explicit named dependency, obligation, patronage relationship, or equivalent source-compatible leverage fact.

The Goblin Leader submits because the camp's useful institutions have become materially better served through the Overlord than through independent rule.

This is not a generic count of three provider quests.

### 4.3 DESTROYED

The native Goblin Champion is the approved principal inner provider or obstacle exposing the military/destructive route.

The Champion may reveal or embody the camp's defensive organization and military vulnerability.

The Leader remains the political authority until the destructive route explicitly permits removal.

The exact objective sequence should use source-backed Goblin combat, engineering, explosives, leadership, or defensive mechanics where meaningful, but must satisfy the universal V5 definition of destroying the anchor polity rather than requiring architectural deletion.
