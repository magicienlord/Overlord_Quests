# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Post-Credits End / Wasteland Authored Blueprint

Status: V5 QUEST BLUEPRINT

Purpose: define the post-ending exploration treatment for the Outer End, Better End Cities, Enderman Overhaul, YUNG's Better End Island and related End extensions as one Wasteland context rather than separate mod questlines.

No final quest names or final dialogue are assigned here. Beat IDs are authoring references.

## 1. Campaign role

This is a Post-Credits Adventure unlocked by completion of the central Ender Dragon resolution.

Established V5 meaning:

- the End is the Cataclysm Dimension / dimensional Wasteland;
- defeating the Ender Dragon stops the active wound and new leakage but does not erase the dimension;
- the outer islands, strange End ecology, Endermen and End structures are native features or long-established consequences within that surviving dimension;
- The Outer End, Better End Cities, YUNG's Better End Island and Enderman Overhaul are absorbed into this setting;
- separate mod identities do not become separate campaign branches;
- the exact mechanism by which the Great Cataclysm created, exposed, or connected this realm remains UNKNOWN and is not solved by this Adventure.

## 2. Availability and sequence-break rule

Normal availability:

- central Ender Dragon defeat complete;
- one-time Gnarl ending presentation complete or already acknowledged;
- established-reign / Post-Credits state active.

If the player legitimately reaches outer-island content before the formal Post-Credits wrapper becomes visible, durable exploration and acquisition evidence may satisfy the relevant beat retrospectively. The player must not be asked to rediscover the same site solely because the quest activated later.

## 3. Authored sequence

### POSTEND-010: Pass beyond the central wound site

Prerequisites:

- central Ender Dragon resolution complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- use the source-valid route from the central End island into the outer-island region, normally an End Gateway;
- establish that the Overlord has deliberately begun exploring beyond the Dragon's former immediate domain.

Purpose:

- separate the central campaign's wound-resolution objective from the surviving dimension beyond it;
- make Post-Credits exploration feel like a new expedition under an established reign rather than unfinished ending cleanup.

### POSTEND-020: Establish the outer ecology

Prerequisite:

- `POSTEND-010` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- reach representative outer-island terrain / biome content beyond the central island;
- encounter at least one meaningful native or extended End ecological feature that demonstrates the dimension continues as a living environment.

Purpose:

- establish that the End is not merely the Dragon arena;
- contextualize Enderman Overhaul and Outer End ecology as inhabitants / expressions of the surviving realm rather than as separate imported cosmologies.

Boundary:

- do not require one example of every Enderman variant, biome, plant, mob, or block;
- exact representative visit signal is technical translation work selected from the installed End extensions.

### POSTEND-030: Penetrate the outer ruins

Prerequisite:

- `POSTEND-020` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- discover and enter one major outer-End ruin / city complex, with an enhanced End City or another source-owned major Outer End structure preferred when available in the validated pack.

Purpose:

- move from ecology into evidence of durable structures and history inside the dimension;
- preserve those structures as native End / Wasteland material without declaring a new civilization solely from architecture.

Lore boundary:

- Gnarl may distinguish the site from the central Dragon arena;
- he must not invent builders, dates or political history where current authority is UNKNOWN.

### POSTEND-040: Return with a major outer-island prize

Prerequisite:

- `POSTEND-030` complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- secure one meaningful source-owned prize associated with deep outer-End exploration;
- Elytra acquisition is the preferred universal proof when it remains the validated major End City expedition reward;
- if the final installed End extensions provide a more stable and equally meaningful durable source milestone, technical translation may use that signal without changing the authored purpose.

Purpose:

- give the Post-Credits expedition a finite endpoint without pretending the surviving dimension has been exhausted or conquered completely;
- establish that the Overlord can now exploit and revisit the outer Wasteland as part of his continuing reign.

Completion state:

- dedicated Post-Credits End exploration Adventure complete;
- the End remains permanently accessible and explorable;
- no territorial-annexation percentage, Enderman reputation, or dimension-completion state is created.

## 4. Native remainder and Ramblings

The following remain native exploration rather than mandatory Questlog checklists:

- every Outer End biome;
- every Enderman Overhaul variant;
- every enhanced End City configuration;
- every End ship;
- every decorative ruin;
- every End-exclusive equipment or material family;
- repeated city raiding;
- arbitrary Enderman kill counts.

Sparse Gnarl or appropriate presenter Ramblings may acknowledge genuinely important discoveries, relics, elite encounters or structures after the authored expedition.

## 5. Relationship to Cataclysm Adventure content

L_Ender's Cataclysm structures and bosses that occur in the End retain their own substantial Adventure interpretation.

This Post-Credits End expedition does not absorb or replace:

- Ruined Citadel / Ender Guardian authored Cataclysm history;
- other Cataclysm structure histories already assigned in `V5_MAJOR_ADVENTURES_AUTHORED_BLUEPRINT.md`.

Overlap is geographic, not quest ownership.

## 6. Technical boundary

Implementation should prefer durable native signals for:

- End Gateway / outer-island entry;
- representative outer biome or structure visits;
- major End City / outer structure entry;
- Elytra or alternate approved major-reward acquisition.

Exact mod registry IDs and objective detector choices are technical translation work.

The V5 blueprint does not authorize production changes yet.
