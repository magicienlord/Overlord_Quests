# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Major Adventures Authored Blueprint

Status: V5 QUEST BLUEPRINT

Purpose: define the substantial optional Adventure arcs whose campaign roles are established, while preserving native progression where it already owns the gameplay sequence.

No final quest names or final dialogue are assigned here. Beat IDs are authoring references.

## 1. Shared rules

1. These Adventures are optional to the central campaign unless a separate V5 authority section explicitly states otherwise.
2. They may begin whenever normal exploration and source prerequisites legitimately expose them after the opening campaign state.
3. They do not contribute to a hidden completion percentage, morality score, or central readiness total.
4. Questlog frames REIGN meaning and remembers authored milestones. It does not duplicate a source mod's complete progression system.
5. Durable native advancements, kill history, structure history and other source-owned state are preferred where they already prove the accomplishment.
6. Early legitimate progress must be recognized when a durable source signal can prove it.
7. A source mod's content does not acquire Cataclysm, Empire, civilization, religious, or Overlord historical meaning merely because it appears in an Adventure arc.
8. Where V5 has not yet selected a player-facing culmination, this document leaves that point explicitly unresolved rather than inheriting an older production choice by default.

## 5. Rats and Ratlantis

Campaign role:

- full Adventure beginning with ordinary rat utility and escalating into discovery of Ratlantis;
- present Ratlantis as an absurdly old, self-contained rat civilization with its own culture and technology;
- preserve the intentionally strange tone without reducing the arc to a joke questline.

Established history:

- Ratlantis is independent of the Cataclysm and the Glorious Empire;
- its antiquity does not make it a hidden predecessor state to known REIGN civilizations;
- no generalized civilization disposition is added to Rats or Ratlantis without a future explicit decision.

### ADV-RATS-010: Put ordinary rats to work

Visible accomplishment:

- tame or otherwise establish ownership/control of an ordinary rat through the source system;
- use at least one meaningful rat utility or automation function.

Purpose:

- make mundane rat handling the opening layer before the Adventure escalates into Ratlantis.

Boundary:

- no exhaustive rat upgrade or automation checklist.

### ADV-RATS-020: Reach Ratlantis

Prerequisite:

- ordinary rat progression has exposed the source-owned route naturally.

Visible accomplishment:

- create/use the native Ratlantis access mechanism and enter Ratlantis.

Purpose:

- shift the arc from utility systems into discovery of the ancient rat civilization.

Implementation boundary:

- Questlog observes real dimension-entry or durable source state and does not recreate the portal mechanic.

### ADV-RATS-030: Establish Ratlantis as a civilization

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-RATS-020` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- acquire representative Ratlantis-native material, artifact or technology;
- explore enough source-owned content to establish that Ratlantis possesses its own culture and technological tradition.

Purpose:

- make the setting discovery meaningful without requiring collection of every Ratlantis item.

Boundary:

- exact representative material/artifact is selected from the installed Rats source during technical translation;
- the selected evidence must be native to Ratlantis and durable enough to observe.

### ADV-RATS-040: Break the Ratlantean powers

Prerequisite:

- `ADV-RATS-030` complete.

Visible accomplishments:

- defeat the Rat Baron through its native encounter;
- defeat the Flying Dutchrat through its native encounter.

Purpose:

- give the full Adventure a concrete confrontation phase against the major Ratlantis powers already exposed by the installed content.

Implementation boundary:

- use persistent source-backed kill history or exact durable encounter signals;
- Questlog does not reproduce either encounter's source mechanics.

### ADV-RATS-050: Conclude the Ratlantis expedition

Prerequisite:

- both authored major Ratlantis confrontation outcomes are complete.

Visible accomplishment:

- retain a durable marker that the Ratlantis Adventure has been completed after its exploration and major confrontations.

Completion state:

- Ratlantis Adventure complete.

Explicit non-consequences:

- completion does not make Ratlantis an OVERLORD REIGN civilization-disposition target;
- completion does not reinterpret its history through the Cataclysm or Empire;
- completion does not require every rat upgrade, Ratlantis recipe or collectible.

## 6. L_Ender's Cataclysm

Campaign role:

- one substantial optional Adventure spanning several ancient sites and major threats;
- demonstrate that the installed Cataclysm content has multiple distinct REIGN historical origins rather than one universal explanation;
- allow legitimate progress before the central End expedition where structures are naturally reachable, then continue into End-linked content and Post-Credits where necessary.

Global rule:

- this is one Adventure, not eight separate historical questlines;
- discovering one Cataclysm structure never proves that all other Cataclysm content shares its origin;
- source-owned structure and boss advancements are the preferred accomplishment signals.

### ADV-CATA-010: Discover that the sites do not share one origin

Visible accomplishment:

- discover any authored Cataclysm structure available through ordinary exploration;
- continue far enough to encounter a second authored site from a different established historical category.

Purpose:

- establish the central interpretive rule of this Adventure: these ruins and creatures are gathered by a mod, not by one REIGN historical cause.

Implementation:

- retrospective source-owned structure advancements may satisfy the relevant discoveries.

### ADV-CATA-020: Ancient Netherworld remnants

Visible accomplishments:

- discover the Soul Forge / Netherite Monstrosity content;
- defeat the Netherite Monstrosity;
- discover the Burning Arena / Ignis content;
- defeat Ignis.

Established interpretations:

- Soul Forge / Netherite Monstrosity belongs to ancient pre-Cataclysm Netherworld industrial/construct infrastructure tied to an older Overlord/Minion era;
- Piglins may occupy similar ruins but did not build this infrastructure;
- Ignis and the Burning Arena belong to an ancient Netherworld champion/warrior tradition independent of the Cataclysm;
- Ignis is never reframed as a secret Overlord.

Relevant source signals include:

- `cataclysm:find_soul_black_smith`;
- `cataclysm:kill_monstrosity`;
- `cataclysm:find_burning_arena`;
- `cataclysm:kill_ignis`.

### ADV-CATA-030: Imperial industry and Ruborian resurrection

Visible accomplishments:

- discover the Ancient Factory and defeat the Harbinger;
- discover the Cursed Pyramid and defeat the Ancient Remnant.

Established interpretations:

- the Ancient Factory / Harbinger is a Glorious Empire military-industrial remnant and was not created by the Cataclysm;
- the Ancient Remnant was originally a Ruborian royal beast and was later resurrected through Cataclysm magic.

Relevant source signals include:

- `cataclysm:find_ancient_factory`;
- `cataclysm:kill_harbinger`;
- `cataclysm:find_cursed_pyramid`;
- `cataclysm:kill_remnant`.

### ADV-CATA-040: Oceanic leakage and older independent ruins

Visible accomplishments:

- discover the Sunken City and defeat the Leviathan;
- discover the Acropolis and defeat Scylla.

Established interpretations:

- the Leviathan / Sunken City represents broad long-term oceanic Cataclysm leakage, parallel to Fathoms and Nerakyss rather than subordinate to either;
- Scylla / Acropolis predates the Cataclysm independently.

Relevant source signals include:

- `cataclysm:find_sunken_city`;
- `cataclysm:kill_leviathan`;
- `cataclysm:find_acropolis`;
- `cataclysm:kill_scylla`.

### ADV-CATA-050: The Frosted Prison

Visible accomplishments:

- discover the Frosted Prison;
- defeat Maledictus.

Established interpretation:

- Maledictus was originally a general appointed to Nordberg by the Fourth Overlord;
- the Fourth Overlord's fall caused or led to his imprisonment;
- he survives in the present as Maledictus.

Required unknowns preserved:

- who imprisoned him;
- the imprisoner's motive;
- the exact mechanism of his transformation.

Relevant source signals include:

- `cataclysm:find_frosted_prison`;
- `cataclysm:kill_maledictus`.

### ADV-CATA-060: The transformed End ruins

Availability:

- becomes naturally reachable when the player has legitimate access to the End / Cataclysm Dimension;
- it may therefore occur during or after the central End expedition and must not be required before that access exists.

Visible accomplishments:

- discover the Ruined Citadel;
- defeat the Ender Guardian.

Established interpretation:

- the Ruined Citadel / Ender Guardian is directly Cataclysmic and belongs to the transformed native End / Wasteland history.

Relevant source signals include:

- `cataclysm:find_ruined_citadel`;
- `cataclysm:kill_ender_guardian`.

### ADV-CATA-070: Interpret the breadth of the record

Prerequisite:

- multiple authored historical categories above have been completed.

Purpose:

- close the historical interpretation of the Adventure by demonstrating that the Overlord has encountered ancient Netherworld remains, imperial or Ruborian material, oceanic effects, independent ancient sites, Nordberg history and transformed End content without collapsing them into one cause.

Presentation boundary:

- Gnarl may interpret what is established and explicitly leave the Maledictus unknowns unresolved;
- no new universal Cataclysm theory is introduced.

### ADV-CATA-080: Adventure capstone

Status:

- REQUIRED V5 DECISION.

Technical option already available:

- the installed source exposes `cataclysm:kill_all_bosses`, which can serve as a clean total-completion capstone without Questlog duplicating every boss counter.

Authored alternative:

- V5 may instead close the REIGN Adventure after a selected breadth of the historically authored strands, leaving additional Cataclysm bosses as native or Post-Credits play.

The capstone must be selected explicitly because choosing all bosses versus selected historical breadth materially changes the intended scope of the authored Adventure.

## 7. Current V5 decision boundary

Two player-facing Adventure decisions remain unresolved in this blueprint:

1. Bumblezone culmination: which one source-owned milestone should close the compact authored arc after meaningful Queen contact?
2. L_Ender's Cataclysm capstone: should the authored Adventure require the native all-bosses completion, or close after a selected breadth of the historically interpreted strands while leaving remaining encounters native?

All other exact advancement IDs, Ratlantis representative items, KnightQuest recipe/state details, source encounter IDs and retrospective detectors are technical translation work unless source inspection exposes a new player-facing choice.
