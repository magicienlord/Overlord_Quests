# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Campaign Coverage Master Index

Status: V5 CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY / COVERAGE AND OWNERSHIP INDEX

Purpose: provide one authoritative map of the V5 campaign so later implementation can determine which blueprint owns a piece of content, when that content becomes available, what it gates, and what is intentionally left to native play or NPC Ramblings.

This file does not replace the detailed authored blueprints. It indexes them and records the latest V5 supersessions of older assignment documents.

No final quest names or final dialogue are assigned here.

## 1. Authority order

For V5 campaign construction, read authority in this order:

```text
latest explicit Overlord approval
-> focused V5 authored blueprint
-> focused V5 campaign authority
-> V5 aggregate authority / this index
-> V5 technical source audits
-> read-only Lore_and_Canon authority not superseded by V5
-> older production quest documentation
-> current production implementation as technical evidence only
```

A mod receiving less Questlog coverage in V5 than in an older assignment file is not a regression when V5 explicitly changed that allocation.

## 2. Campaign topology

The authored campaign topology is:

```text
Awakening / Tower summit
-> Master's Staff + first Brown proof
-> semi-open initial Dark Tower restoration
   + Red -> Green -> Blue Minion restoration
   + parallel optional Adventures
   + parallel Civilizations
   + magic mastery as rooms become operational
   + farming / automation
   + personal / conditional side arcs
-> all fourteen initial Tower functions operational
   AND all four Minion tribes restored
-> silent readiness gate
-> five concurrent Bosses'Rise subcampaigns
-> short Gnarl synthesis / preparation transition
-> Cataclysm Dimension expedition
-> Ender Dragon
-> one-time Gnarl ending
-> established reign
-> Post-Credits exploration and later Adventures
```

Civilizations and optional Adventures do not gate the central ending unless a later focused V5 authority explicitly says otherwise.

## 9. Conditional and sequence-break treatment

V5 assumes a fresh single-player world but still recognizes legitimate early accomplishments where durable source truth exists.

Required behavior:

- do not ask the player to repeat a boss kill, native advancement, structure discovery or other durable accomplishment solely because an authored quest became visible later;
- preserve the authored interpretation and presentation when retrospective completion occurs;
- do not add multiplayer, late-install backfill or generalized recovery architecture merely to solve hypothetical unsupported play histories.

Conditional arcs:

- Pet Cemetery appears only after an owned supported pet dies;
- NightWalker appears only after actual completed vampirism;
- civilization roots appear after deliberate anchor selection;
- Dragon Den appears after dragon mastery;
- dedicated Post-Credits End exploration appears chiefly after the central ending.

L_Ender's Cataclysm may begin before the central End expedition and continue after the credits.

## 10. Popup / Rambling ownership

Ramblings are a separate presentation allocation, not miniature questlines.

They are primarily used for:

- significant native advancements deliberately left outside Questlog;
- first notable discoveries;
- meaningful boss / elite defeats not assigned their own quest beat;
- later opposite-path mastery acknowledgements;
- major relic or system milestones;
- historical context that is better delivered at the corresponding native event than inside a broad quest description.

A dedicated V5 Ramblings authority governs the exact use rules and known required / eligible groups.

Ramblings must remain sparse. They do not create hidden completion scores or reopen completed arcs.

## 15. Coverage-complete test

V5 campaign coverage is structurally complete only when all of the following are true:

- every central gate has an authored owner;
- every dedicated questline allocation has a beat graph;
- every V5-added dedicated / conditional arc has a beat graph;
- all eleven civilizations have terminal route graphs;
- all Tower-readiness functions have restoration beats;
- every deliberate V5 exclusion is recorded as native, absorbed, Rambling-only or no-treatment;
- the presenter system can derive a finite final speaking-character roster;
- no earlier assignment can silently reintroduce a superseded questline;
- remaining UNKNOWN items are either deliberate lore mysteries or narrow implementation details, not missing campaign decisions.

At this stage, the major structural ownership graph is complete. Remaining V5 work is specification closure: Ramblings allocation, presenter-roster closure, selected exact objective actions where campaign meaning depends on them, and a final unresolved-decision audit.

The V5 campaign authority still does not authorize production implementation.
