# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Piglin Civilization Blueprint

Status: WORKING V5 CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY

Date: 2026-09-16

Authority relationship: this document is a focused civilization blueprint under `docs/V5_CAMPAIGN_SYSTEM_AUTHORITY.md` and `docs/V5_CIVILIZATION_SYSTEM_AUTHORITY.md`. It records explicit V5 decisions approved by the Overlord and source-backed mechanics from the exact installed Nether Villages artifact and vanilla Piglin systems.

## 1. Canonical anchor

The canonical Piglin polity is one runtime-selected Nether Village.

The village is Piglin-built territory. It is not abandoned Minion infrastructure and it does not represent every Piglin settlement, Bastion or Piglin population in the Netherworld.

A native Piglin Brute inside the selected anchor receives the authored local title:

```text
Chieftain
```

The Chieftain is the civilization starter and local political authority.

The exact eligibility filters for selecting the village and binding the Brute are technical details for the objective/provider pass. The selection must remain deliberate under the universal civilization anchor rule rather than occurring through proximity, incidental combat or ordinary bartering.

## 2. Political spine

The approved Piglin political problem is territorial inheritance versus the returning Master.

Piglins expanded through the Netherworld during the Silence while Minion numbers and territorial control declined. The canonical Nether Village is therefore genuinely Piglin-built and Piglin-held territory in the present age.

At the same time:

- Piglins descend from an ancient Minion and pig union;
- Piglins and Minions recognize one another as unusually close Netherworld cousin-peoples;
- Gnarl knows the ancestry and is embarrassed by it;
- the returning Overlord represents the restoration of a power that once dominated much more of the Netherworld.

The ancestry does not create automatic ownership. The political question is whether the Overlord recognizes the independence that developed during the Silence, establishes new dominion over the village, or destroys its polity.

## 3. Native political ingredients

V5 uses the exact source-owned Piglin systems rather than inventing a parallel profession or reputation layer.

Relevant native ingredients include:

```text
gold bartering
gold-armor tolerance for ordinary Piglins
Piglin Brute hostility
anger over protected containers
anger over mining local gold or gold-related blocks
ordinary Piglin group response to player aggression
```

The installed Nether Villages mod contributes visible civic and resource infrastructure, including forge, mine, storage, farm, tower and residential pieces.

Those structures are village functions, not invented Piglin professions.

## 4. NEUTRAL route

The independent route recognizes the legitimacy of the polity the Piglins built during the Silence.

Approved structure:

```text
anchor locked
-> learn the canonical village's gold, barter and protected-property customs
-> establish a useful exchange relationship through source-native Piglin systems
-> establish that the village is a present Piglin polity rather than inherited Minion infrastructure
-> final settlement with Chieftain
-> explicitly recognize Piglin possession and political independence of the canonical village
-> Chieftain recognizes the Overlord as the returned Netherworld power, but not as his sovereign
-> NEUTRAL
```

This is affirmative coexistence. Ordinary successful bartering or wearing gold armor does not write `NEUTRAL` by itself.

## 5. SUBJUGATED route

The Overlord establishes dominion over a people who became independent during the Silence.

Approved pressure points:

```text
gold access
barter wealth
village resource infrastructure
Minion-Piglin kinship as political context, not automatic ownership
```

Approved structure:

```text
anchor locked
-> learn the village's gold and property order
-> gain meaningful control, access or indispensable leverage over the economic foundations the Piglins value
-> demonstrate authority over relevant gold, barter and village-resource flows through exact source-backed objectives
-> invoke the restored Master's Netherworld power and the Piglins' Minion kinship as context
-> confront Chieftain with the village's practical inability or unwillingness to exclude the Overlord
-> Chieftain accepts that the returned Master stands above this part of the Netherworld
-> SUBJUGATED
```

The ancestry is supporting political context only. V5 must not state that Piglin descent from a Minion means the Overlord automatically owned the canonical village before the authored submission.

### 5.1 Required terminal gameplay consequences

Once the canonical village is `SUBJUGATED`:

- it is safe for the Overlord regardless of gold armor;
- local protected containers may be accessed without native retaliation;
- local gold and gold-related resources may be extracted without native retaliation;
- native barter and approved village services remain usable;
- authored tribute may be represented where the detailed provider/objective pass gives it a concrete source-compatible form.

These consequences apply to the canonical anchor. They do not globally pacify every Piglin or Bastion.

## 6. DESTROYED route

The destructive route rejects Piglin ownership and deliberately breaks the canonical village's political order.

Approved structure:

```text
choose destructive route
-> deliberately violate the village's protected gold/property order through authored objectives
-> trigger or accept the resulting native hostility
-> defeat the Chieftain
-> remove any additional civilization-specific defensive or civic function proven indispensable by the final source-backed objective pass
-> canonical Nether Village ceases to function as the authored Piglin polity
-> DESTROYED
```

The exact indispensable targets beyond the Chieftain remain unresolved until the source/objective pass proves what is mechanically and politically meaningful.

V5 must not replace this with a generic Piglin kill quota or require every village block to be destroyed.

Other Piglins, Bastions and Nether Villages remain extant.

## 7. Provider discipline

The installed village mod does not add Piglin professions or a reputation framework.

Therefore V5 must not invent a Piglin blacksmith, farmer, miner or similar NPC class merely because a forge, farm or mine exists in the village.

The recurring political cast may remain compact, centered on the Chieftain plus source-native Piglins or Brutes only where an exact objective or provider function requires them.

Village structures themselves may carry economic meaning through block, item, barter, mining or access objectives without being personified as unsupported offices.

## 8. Technical objective rule

Native Piglin behaviors are implementation ingredients, not terminal-state detectors.

For example:

- successful barter can prove exchange but does not prove independence or submission;
- opening a protected container can be a deliberate destructive or dominance action only when the authored route is active;
- mining local gold can have political meaning only when scoped to the canonical anchor and the correct route;
- ordinary Piglin aggression elsewhere must not alter the canonical village disposition.

Where vanilla statistics or advancements are too broad to prove anchor-local intent, later implementation may use a narrow Questlog compatibility bridge scoped to the canonical village and exact action.
