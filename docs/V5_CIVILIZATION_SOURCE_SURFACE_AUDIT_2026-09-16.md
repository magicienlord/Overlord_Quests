# OVERLORD REIGN V5 Civilization Source Surface Audit

Status: VERIFIED TECHNICAL FACT / V5 SUPPORTING AUDIT

Date: 2026-09-16

Purpose: identify source-owned mechanics that can support authored V5 civilization routes without allowing native progression to invent political meaning. This file is evidence, not campaign-route authority.

## 1. Audit rule

A native advancement, item, structure, trade, boss, or interaction is only an implementation ingredient.

It becomes part of a civilization route only after V5 establishes why that action matters politically to the canonical anchor.

The audit therefore separates:

- durable technical signals;
- useful native cultural/gameplay surfaces;
- already-established REIGN political authority;
- remaining campaign-design gaps that must return to the Overlord.

## 3. Villagers

Established REIGN authority:

- decentralized human civilization;
- canonical civilization arc uses one designated authored anchor;
- all compatible Villager professions may provide sidequests when appropriate;
- Guard Villagers are preferred native physical defense for hostile local outcomes;
- recurring human tension is productive domination versus destructive indulgence;
- exact anchor history remains not fully fixed. Spree is a strong candidate, but older authority permits another inherited human site according to final-world fit.

Technical surface:

- VillagersPlus adds Alchemist, Miner, Occultist, Oceanographer and Horticulturist profession infrastructure;
- its 18 packaged advancements are recipe unlocks, not accomplishment signals;
- Guard Villagers provides native local defense without requiring a new army simulation.

V5 gap:

The source does not yet establish the one local political problem whose three inner routes resolve the canonical human anchor. The exact historical anchor is also still open under older authority.

## 4. Illagers

Established REIGN authority:

- designated Take a Pillage Bastille is the anchor;
- opening is hostile;
- authority is established through force/intimidation;
- breaking Bastille authority may create a `COWED` intermediary phase;
- keeping the Illagers useful may lead to submission;
- local destruction remains possible;
- subjugation does not pacify unrelated Illager groups or change their hostility toward Villagers.

Technical surface:

- native location advancement identifies the Bastille;
- Bastille structure contains military, prisoner, ravager, farm, church and graveyard infrastructure;
- Ravager Horn can feed vanilla Bad Omen / raid infrastructure as a low-overhead subjugation benefit;
- Take a Pillage provides Archer, Skirmisher, Legioner and related hostile combat roles.

V5 gap:

The opening and destructive logic are strongly constrained, but the affirmative terminal `NEUTRAL` settlement and exact authored act of `SUBJUGATED` submission are not yet fully specified.

## 5. Dwarves

Established REIGN authority:

- canonical anchor is a Golden Hills / Golden Halls successor hold;
- Forge-Thane is the local ruler and quest starter;
- one designated NPC retains serious historical records;
- amethyst is current internal exchange material;
- gold remains heritage, prestige, treasure and ancestral fixation;
- subjugation may provide safe access, better/unlocked trades, runes/equipment, tribute and services;
- Dwarf-Kobold rivalry remains active and may feed an optional shared chain.

Technical surface:

- packaged advancements are recipe unlocks only;
- exact crafted-item statistics can durably prove selected Dwarven smithing/rune outputs;
- native Villager-derived reputation pricing can support authored trade consequences without a new continuous pricing simulation.

V5 gap:

Source material provides excellent economic/craft leverage but does not yet establish the local crisis or pressure point that makes the Forge-Thane choose independence, submission, or destruction.

## 6. Gnumus

Established REIGN authority:

- principal Large Gnumus Settlement is the anchor;
- Elder Shaman is the main political starter;
- regional Gnumus descend from Gluttony-altered Halflings;
- the local Gnumus do not know this;
- ancestry reveal/withhold is an approved remembered decision contained inside the one civilization arc.

Exact native structures include:

- `large_gnumus_settlement`;
- `gnumus_settlement`;
- `small_gnumus_settlement`;
- `shamans_hut`;
- `traders_rest`;
- farms, ruins, camp, bonfire and statue structures.

Useful exact native advancement/actions include:

- `business_approach`: trade with a Gnumus Merchant using Gnumus Doubloons;
- `plan_boar`: kill Big Snout;
- `vintage_blacksmith`: make Vintage Alloy Ingot;
- `vintage_improvement`: upgrade a Gnumus Pickaxe or Cutlass to Vintage;
- `vintage_technology`: make the Vintage Universal Tool;
- `hearty_dish`: make Hearty Dinner;
- `weight_loss_protection`: obtain Gnumus Totem;
- `worthy_traders_hat`: obtain Business Hat;
- additional hunting, food, craft and local-object interactions.

The Merchant implementation also owns specific trade behavior and source-awarded progression rather than requiring inventory inference.

V5 gap:

The source supplies multiple plausible provider chains, but nothing technical determines which local problem each chain solves or which chain reveals `NEUTRAL`, `SUBJUGATED`, or `DESTROYED`. The exact placement of the ancestry reveal inside that political progression also remains an authoring decision.

## 7. Goblins

Established REIGN authority:

- one major Goblin Camp is the canonical anchor;
- a designated `leader_goblin` is the political starter;
- other camps remain independent;
- Goblins and Minions culturally view one another as something like distant cousins, without canonized shared biology;
- submission should preserve merchants, shamans, engineers, craftsmen, tavern roles and other useful services.

Useful exact native advancement/actions include:

- find a Goblin structure;
- craft/use Goblin disguise content;
- purchase from Merchant;
- purchase liquor from Bartender;
- trade Gold Coin with Wanderer;
- give Mushroom Stew to Merchant;
- use Engineer Workbench;
- obtain and upgrade Goblin Prototype;
- wake a sleeping drunk Goblin;
- tame/equip Glitteron;
- obtain/use several bomb types;
- `goblins_slayer_success`, explicitly framed by the source as `We do not come in peace`.

Native roles include Leader, Champion, Merchant, Engineer, Engineeress, Blacksmith, Shaman, Bard, Bartender, Huntsman, Hunter, Knight, Wanderer and several cave-Goblin roles.

V5 gap:

The mod supplies unusually rich provider material but does not define the REIGN political crisis of the canonical camp. Disguise, engineering, tavern, prototype and merchant progression are ingredients, not yet authorized route meanings.

## 8. Kobolds

Established REIGN authority:

- designated Kobold Den is the anchor;
- protected Kobold Captain is the starter;
- Engineers and Enchanters are sharper specialists within generally low-intelligence Kobold society;
- Pirate Kobolds remain politically independent unless an authored quest connects them;
- submission preserves Engineer, Enchanter, Captain, Warrior, trade/resource and tribute benefits;
- optional Dwarf-Kobold rivalry may alter later route options without directly setting disposition.

Technical surface:

- exact JAR contains no advancement definitions;
- native Captain/specialist transactions are AI-driven and do not expose a sufficiently specific durable player statistic;
- a narrow transaction bridge is justified only when V5 actually requires one;
- the mod provides Den, Pirate and underground structural content plus Captain, Warrior, Engineer, Enchanter, Pirate and Rascal roles.

V5 gap:

The native mod defines social roles and services but not the political dispute that produces the three terminal routes.

## 9. Ribbits

Established REIGN authority:

- designated Ribbit Village is the anchor;
- Gardener assigned local role of Elder is the starter;
- Ribbits are source-native peaceful, cozy, musical, cheerful and slightly silly;
- domination/destruction should gain its dark comedy from contrast with that innocence;
- submission can preserve Merchant, Fisherman, Sorcerer, agricultural and local services.

Technical surface:

- six packaged advancements are recipe unlocks only;
- Ribbits use vanilla Merchant behavior, but the vanilla trade statistic is too broad to prove a Ribbit-specific transaction;
- if V5 requires a specific Ribbit trade action, use a narrow filtered bridge;
- village structural pieces include Gardener, Fisherman, Sorcerer, Merchant and related local buildings.

V5 gap:

No source-backed local crisis currently determines the three political routes. V5 must avoid inventing a grim military problem merely to manufacture stakes.

## 10. Sea Dwellers

Established REIGN authority:

- designated Sea Village is the anchor;
- one senior trader assigned the title `Sea Elder` is the political starter;
- other Sea Villages remain independent;
- submission may preserve traders, underwater equipment/resources, Aquamarine commerce, tribute and local services.

Useful exact native advancement/actions include:

- Aquamarine barter;
- fish barter;
- Nautilus barter with Collector;
- make Depth Ingot;
- open a casket;
- use profession Seashells;
- turn a Villager into a Mermorph;
- break a Sea Lantern and enrage nearby Mermorphs.

Verified native cultural enforcement:

- `minecraft:sea_lantern` is the exact `seadwellers:mermorph_aggro_blocks` tag member;
- breaking one within the native system applies Mermorph Rage for 6000 ticks;
- attacking a Mermorph triggers rage;
- killing a Mermorph triggers a much longer rage state of 36000 ticks.

V5 gap:

The source establishes trade culture, mutable professions, property taboo and retaliation very well, but it does not determine the authored political problem whose final resolution is independence, submission or destruction.

## 11. Piglins

Established REIGN authority:

- canonical anchor is one designated Nether Village;
- Chieftain role is layered onto one protected Piglin Brute;
- ordinary Piglins retain native gold-armor tolerance outside authored exceptions;
- Piglins expanded during the Silence as Minion dominance receded;
- Piglins and Minions are close Netherworld cousin-peoples;
- submission must make the canonical village safe regardless of gold armor and permit the Overlord to loot containers/mine local gold without native retaliation;
- other Piglin populations remain native.

Technical surface:

- Nether Villages supplies the authored settlement substrate;
- vanilla Piglin gold, barter, container anger and mining anger mechanics provide meaningful native pressure without a new reputation system.

V5 gap:

The Chieftain's exact political problem and the authored acts that distinguish independent peace from submission are not yet fixed.

## 12. Umvuthana

Established REIGN authority is already unusually specific:

- canonical Grove is centered on one Umvuthi;
- Umvuthi is local creator-god and ruler;
- native hostility remains before legitimate audience;
- Umvuthana mask is the source-backed peaceful approach mechanism;
- submission should humiliate a living creator-god into acknowledging the Overlord as greater power;
- killing the canonical Umvuthi is the local destructive resolution;
- other Groves remain independent.

Useful exact native signals:

- `mowziesmobs:sneak_grove`: source-owned disguised Grove infiltration;
- `mowziesmobs:suns_blessing`: receive Sun's Blessing;
- `mowziesmobs:kill_umvuthi`: kill Umvuthi;
- `mowziesmobs:kill_umvuthana_raptor`: kill Raptor.

V5 gap:

The source nearly determines the outer skeleton. What remains is the exact affirmative `NEUTRAL` settlement and the exact authored act that forces Umvuthi's submission without conflating submission with killing him.

## 13. Myrmex

The current Myrmex political mapping is already approved in `V5_ICE_AND_FIRE_CAMPAIGN_AUTHORITY.md`:

- `NEUTRAL`: authored resolution with native opinion floor 50;
- `SUBJUGATED`: authored resolution with native opinion floor 75;
- `DESTROYED`: authored destruction of canonical hive/Queen through a source-backed route;
- native opinion thresholds support inner progression but never automatically choose terminal political state.

Remaining work is exact quest-route authoring, not state-system design.

## 14. Audit conclusion

The eleven civilizations are not equally underdefined.

High political-source constraint:

- Illagers;
- Umvuthana;
- Myrmex.

Moderate constraint with strong cultural/economic mechanics but unresolved political spine:

- Dwarves;
- Gnumus;
- Goblins;
- Piglins;
- Sea Dwellers.

Structurally/culturally defined but politically underauthored:

- Villagers;
- Kobolds;
- Ribbits.

The next V5 civilization pass must therefore ask narrowly about political problems and route meanings, not ask the Overlord to restate native mechanics or already-established lore.
